# Raleigh Lead Recovery: Dead-Lead Reactivation Action Plan

Last updated: 2026-08-18

## The decision

Reposition Raleigh Lead Recovery around one outcome:

> Turn the leads you already paid for into booked jobs.

The initial niche should be **roofing companies in the Triangle**. Roofing has the right economics for a focused test: expensive leads, high job values, seasonal follow-up gaps, and large databases of estimates and inquiries that did not close.

Keep the current missed-call product working, but do not make it the primary homepage or outbound story. It becomes a future add-on: recover old leads first, then prevent new leads from going cold.

## What Raleigh Lead Recovery is selling

Do not lead with AI, Vapi, automation, or a replacement for the prospect's CRM.

The offer is a controlled reactivation pilot:

- The customer supplies 250-500 old leads from an agreed period.
- Mabel calls only records that pass the customer's consent, suppression, and do-not-call review.
- Mabel identifies still-interested homeowners, qualifies the opportunity, and books or hands off the next step.
- The customer receives a result report: records attempted, connections, conversations, interested leads, qualified opportunities, appointments, opt-outs, and attributable pipeline.
- No new ad spend and no CRM replacement.

For the first three design partners, use a tightly capped pilot in exchange for feedback, permission to publish anonymized results, and—only if the result is strong—a named case study. Do not promise a revenue number before real performance data exists. After those pilots, set a fixed pilot price or usage fee based on actual call cost, labor, conversion rate, and sales value.

Do **not** use an AI voice to cold-call a scraped prospect list. The first AI sales call should happen only after the recipient requests it through a form, email reply, or another documented opt-in path. Before Mabel calls a customer's old consumer leads, have qualified counsel approve the exact consent, disclosure, DNC/suppression, recording, calling-hours, caller-ID, and record-retention workflow. The customer must contractually confirm that each uploaded list is eligible for the planned outreach. This is a launch gate, not a later cleanup task.

## Workstream 1: lock the offer and proof model

### Deliverables

- One-line promise: `Turn the leads you already paid for into booked jobs.`
- Niche statement: `Lead reactivation for roofing companies.`
- Pilot definition: lead count, list age, campaign length, call attempts, handoff method, calendar rules, and success measures.
- Qualification rules for a good customer:
  - Has at least 500 old leads in a CRM or exportable system.
  - Buys leads or actively runs paid search/social.
  - Has a real person who can take qualified handoffs and follow up quickly.
  - Has enough average job value to make recovered opportunities meaningful.
  - Can document the source and outreach eligibility of the supplied records.
- Pilot scorecard with no invented benchmarks.

### Success measures

Track the full funnel, but use **qualified appointments per 100 eligible records** as the primary operational metric. Also track contact rate, meaningful conversation rate, interested rate, booking rate, opt-out rate, cost per qualified appointment, pipeline accepted by the customer, and closed revenue later.

## Workstream 2: reposition the landing page

The page should make one argument and drive one primary action: request a Mabel call.

### Recommended above-the-fold copy

**Eyebrow:** Lead reactivation for roofing companies

**Headline:** Turn the leads you've already paid for into booked jobs.

**Subheadline:** Mabel follows up with old and unresponsive homeowners, finds the ones who are still interested, and sends qualified opportunities back to your team.

**Primary CTA:** Have Mabel Call Me

**CTA support:** Enter your number and experience the same call your old leads would receive.

**Secondary CTA:** See the Pilot

`We turn dead leads into new business` is a strong campaign line and a good headline variant. The recommended control is more specific about the customer's sunk lead cost and the desired outcome.

### Page structure

1. Outcome-led hero and opt-in call form.
2. The economic problem: the CRM contains leads the company already paid to acquire.
3. Simple flow: upload eligible old leads → Mabel has conversations → the team receives qualified opportunities.
4. Pilot offer with clear boundaries and no CRM replacement.
5. Sample results dashboard labeled as an example until real results exist.
6. Call recording or interactive demo with explicit AI disclosure.
7. Objections: list security, brand control, handoff process, opt-outs/compliance, integrations, and pricing.
8. Founder trust section focused on local implementation and support; remove the Raleigh AI Guy positioning from the main conversion path.
9. Repeat the `Have Mabel Call Me` CTA.

### Product changes implied by the new page

- Replace the current signup-first CTA with an explicit phone consent form containing name, company, business email, phone, consent language, timestamp, source page, and request ID.
- Trigger one requested demo call immediately or at a selected time; provide a cancel/opt-out path.
- Change dashboard and sample-report language from inbound missed calls to reactivation attempts, connections, conversations, qualified leads, appointments, and pipeline.
- Preserve existing inbound assistant provisioning separately instead of forcing the new offer through the current missed-call onboarding.

## Workstream 3: build the first prospect list

Start with **50 Triangle roofing companies**, researched manually. Do not buy or scrape thousands of records yet.

### Geography

Raleigh, Cary, Apex, Garner, Wake Forest, Durham, Chapel Hill, Holly Springs, Fuquay-Varina, Clayton, and nearby Triangle markets.

### Required fit signals

Prioritize companies with at least three of these signals:

- Active Google Local Services Ads, Google Ads, or Meta advertising.
- Dedicated estimate/quote landing pages.
- Residential storm, replacement, or insurance-claim work.
- Meaningful review volume and evidence of multiple crews or locations.
- A visible CRM, booking, chat, or lead-capture workflow.
- Hiring for sales representatives, appointment setters, or canvassers.
- An owner, GM, sales manager, or marketing manager who is reachable.
- Likely access to 500+ aged web, estimate, or canvassing leads.

Exclude one-person roof repair businesses, national/franchise branches with unreachable corporate buying, companies without a usable website or local footprint, and companies that cannot establish outreach eligibility for their old records.

## Workstream 4: replace the Smartlead campaign

Do not overwrite the existing Raleigh AI Guy campaign. Pause it, export/preserve its performance history and suppression list, then create a new Raleigh Lead Recovery campaign.

### Campaign setup

- Campaign name: `Roofing — Dead Lead Reactivation — Triangle — V1`
- Segment A: owners/GMs at smaller multi-crew roofing companies.
- Segment B: sales/marketing leaders at larger local roofers.
- Plain-text emails, no images, attachments, or first-touch links.
- Four touches over roughly 12-16 days.
- One low-friction CTA per email.
- Keep sending-domain health, mailbox volume, reply handling, address/opt-out requirements, and suppression lists intact.

### Message sequence

1. **Observation + question:** ask what happens to estimates or web leads the team never reaches or never closes.
2. **Economic angle:** they already paid for those leads; Mabel works a controlled sample without replacing the CRM.
3. **Experience angle:** offer to have Mabel call them so they can hear the exact experience.
4. **Close the loop:** ask whether old-lead reactivation is simply not a priority.

The desired first reply is not a meeting. It is either `yes, show me` or an answer about how they currently handle old leads. When a prospect wants the call, send them to the consent form or collect an explicit requested number and time, log it, and then trigger Mabel.

### Campaign metrics

Ignore opens as the main decision signal. Track delivered, total replies, positive replies, qualified accounts, demo-call opt-ins, completed demo calls, meetings booked, pilots offered, pilots started, and opt-outs/complaints. Review results after the first 25 and 50 researched contacts before expanding the list.

## Workstream 5: build two separate AI agents

### A. Mabel — opt-in sales demo

Purpose: demonstrate the product, uncover lead volume and pain, and book a founder call. It should not attempt to close a complex contract.

Opening:

> Hi {{first_name}}, this is Mabel, Raleigh Lead Recovery's AI calling assistant. You asked me to call so you could hear how the experience works. Is now still a good time for a two-minute demo?

Core questions, one at a time:

1. About how many web or estimate leads go cold in a normal month?
2. What usually happens after your team stops following up?
3. Are those records in a CRM that can be exported?
4. If Mabel found an interested homeowner, should it book an inspection, transfer the call, or send the opportunity to a rep?
5. Would it be useful to map a small pilot with Danny?

Required behaviors:

- Clearly disclose that Mabel is AI.
- Confirm the call is still wanted before continuing.
- Keep the call to about two minutes unless the prospect is actively engaging.
- Explain the outcome in plain language; do not give a Vapi/AI feature tour.
- Never promise conversion rates, appointments, or revenue.
- Handle `not interested`, `remove me`, wrong number, and reschedule immediately.
- Book an approved calendar slot, transfer to Danny only during defined hours, or send a follow-up link with permission.
- Capture call outcome, estimated monthly lead volume, CRM/exportability, preferred handoff, timeline, objections, and meeting status.

### B. Mabel — customer lead-reactivation agent

Purpose: contact an eligible old lead on behalf of a named roofing company, determine current interest, qualify the project, and execute the customer's approved next step.

It needs customer-specific configuration for identity/disclosure, lead source and context, services, territory, qualification questions, insurance/storm rules, calendar availability, transfer rules, voicemail, retry limits, opt-out handling, and prohibited claims.

Minimum dispositions:

- no answer
- voicemail
- wrong number
- do not call
- not interested
- project completed / chose competitor
- interested later
- interested now
- qualified
- appointment booked
- live transfer
- follow-up required

Every opt-out must immediately suppress future attempts across the campaign. Recordings, transcripts, summaries, and structured fields must follow the approved privacy and retention policy.

## Workstream 6: technical implementation

Reuse the existing Vapi webhook, transcript, recording, Supabase, and dashboard foundations, but model reactivation as a separate campaign domain rather than pretending each outbound attempt is an inbound missed call.

### New core records

- `campaigns`: customer, purpose, status, limits, schedule, script version.
- `campaign_contacts`: source record, eligibility attestation, consent/suppression state, personalization fields, disposition.
- `call_attempts`: Vapi call ID, attempt number, timestamps, outcome, cost, recording/transcript references.
- `appointments`: contact, booked time, calendar destination, confirmation status.
- `suppression_entries`: phone/email, reason, source, timestamp, scope.
- `demo_call_requests`: prospect consent text/version, phone, timestamp, IP/source, status.

### Integrations and controls

- Vapi outbound API or campaigns for approved demo and customer calls.
- Calendar booking plus optional live transfer.
- Smartlead reply/webhook handling or a manual consent queue for V1.
- Per-campaign calling windows and timezone checks.
- Attempt caps, pacing, voicemail rules, idempotency, and emergency stop.
- Customer list upload with validation, duplicate removal, phone normalization, suppression checks, and eligibility attestation.
- A preflight preview showing exactly who will be called and why before launch.
- Dashboard funnel and per-lead audit trail.

## 30-day execution order

### Days 1-3: decisions and guardrails

- Lock roofing as the first niche and approve the one-line promise.
- Define the first design-partner pilot and scorecard.
- Get counsel to define the allowed opt-in sales-call flow and customer-list eligibility workflow.
- Decide the handoff: calendar booking first, live transfer second.

**Gate:** no automated outbound call launches until consent, suppression, disclosure, and calling rules are documented.

### Days 4-7: conversion assets

- Rewrite the landing page around lead reactivation.
- Add the `Have Mabel Call Me` consent form.
- Create the demo-call confirmation and fallback scheduling flow.
- Create a reactivation-style example report, clearly labeled as sample data.
- Write and QA Smartlead V1.

**Gate:** five internal testers should understand the offer and successfully request, receive, and complete a Mabel demo call.

### Days 8-12: agent and data foundation

- Build the opt-in sales demo assistant and structured call analysis.
- Add the demo request, campaign, contact, attempt, appointment, and suppression data model.
- Add rate limits, calling windows, attempt limits, opt-out handling, and an emergency stop.
- Run scripted tests for happy path, objection, wrong number, voicemail, reschedule, opt-out, and tool failure.

**Gate:** 20 internal test calls with no missed opt-outs, false promises, incorrect bookings, or broken handoffs.

### Days 13-17: first prospect list

- Research and score 50 Triangle roofing companies.
- Verify 1-2 appropriate decision-makers per company.
- Write one problem-connected personalization observation per A prospect.
- Import only the first 25 verified contacts into Smartlead.

### Days 18-24: controlled outreach

- Launch the first 25 contacts at low volume.
- Danny manually handles replies and makes human follow-up calls.
- Mabel calls only people who explicitly request the demo.
- Review call transcripts and objections daily; tune the prompt by version, not ad hoc.
- Launch the next 25 only after reviewing positive reply and complaint quality.

### Days 25-30: convert a design partner

- Run discovery with qualified accounts.
- Obtain a small, eligible sample export before promising a full pilot.
- Validate list quality and suppression rules.
- Launch one tightly capped design-partner campaign.
- Produce the first real result report and use it to set pricing and revise the offer.

## Ownership

- **Danny:** niche/offer approval, legal review relationship, sales calls, pricing, design-partner close, and customer-specific business rules.
- **Raleigh Lead Recovery app:** consent capture, demo-call triggering, campaign records, suppression, call logs, appointments, and result reporting.
- **Smartlead:** prospect email sequencing and reply generation—not proof of phone consent by itself unless the reply explicitly requests the call and is logged.
- **Vapi:** real-time conversation and outbound call execution after the app authorizes an eligible call.

## What not to do yet

- Do not attack roofing, HVAC, solar, real estate, insurance, and med spas at once.
- Do not rewrite every product screen before one pilot validates the workflow.
- Do not buy a 2,000-company list before the first 50 reveal which signals correlate with replies.
- Do not let the AI cold-call scraped numbers.
- Do not claim recovered revenue from appointments alone.
- Do not hide that the caller is AI.
- Do not let a customer upload and dial a list without eligibility attestation, suppression checks, and a preflight review.

## Definition of success for this phase

Within 30 days:

- The landing page and Smartlead campaign tell the same lead-reactivation story.
- 50 high-fit Triangle roofers are researched and scored.
- At least 25 verified contacts receive the controlled V1 sequence.
- Every AI sales call is tied to a recorded request.
- The sales demo agent completes the flow reliably and books correctly.
- One eligible design-partner pilot is ready to run or running.
- Reporting can show attempts → conversations → qualified leads → appointments without fabricated ROI.
