# Mabel Roofing Demo Scenarios

Use one of these as the homeowner when testing **Mabel — Lead Reactivation Demo** in Vapi. Keep the best call under three minutes and listen back before publishing it.

## Recommended homepage recording: delayed replacement

You requested a roof-replacement estimate eight months ago. You postponed the project because the timing was bad, but a recent leak has made it relevant again. You are open to a roofer calling tomorrow afternoon.

Useful answers:

- The roof is about 18 years old.
- The issue is a leak near the back bedroom after heavy rain.
- You want to understand repair versus replacement.
- Tomorrow afternoon is the preferred callback window.

This scenario demonstrates reactivation, qualification, and a clean handoff without relying on an insurance claim.

## Alternate: storm damage

You asked about an inspection after a storm several months ago but never scheduled it. The project is still open, and you now have your insurance paperwork. You want a roofer to call this week.

Mabel should not interpret coverage, promise that insurance will pay, or invent inspection availability.

## Alternate: timing changed

You requested an estimate last year but delayed the work while preparing to move. You have decided to stay in the home and want to revisit the project in the next 30–60 days.

Mabel should confirm the project type and timing, then demonstrate how the roofing team would receive the opportunity.

## Required quality checks

- Mabel identifies herself as an automated assistant.
- The call is explicitly described as a recorded demonstration.
- Mabel asks one question at a time and does not pressure the homeowner.
- No real appointment, price, insurance outcome, or business availability is invented.
- The close clearly says what the roofing team would receive in a real campaign.
- Everyone on the recording has approved publication.
