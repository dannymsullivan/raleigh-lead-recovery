# Smartlead sequence — Mabel feedback campaign

## Campaign settings

- Send as plain text.
- Disable open tracking and link tracking.
- Use the direct URL exactly as written; do not shorten or wrap it.
- Keep all follow-ups in the same thread.
- Stop the sequence on any reply.
- Start at 5 new contacts per day from `danny@meetraleighaiguy.com`.
- Import only ZeroBounce-verified contacts.

## Email 1 — Day 0

Subject: `quick opinion`

Hi {{first_name}},

I’m building an AI calling assistant that follows up with older leads and identifies the people who are still interested.

I’m asking a few Raleigh business owners to try the experience on their own phone and tell me what sounds natural—or awkward.

https://www.raleighleadrecovery.com/#try-mabel

No CRM access or lead list needed. I’d genuinely appreciate your reaction.

Danny

If this is not relevant, reply no and I will not follow up.
