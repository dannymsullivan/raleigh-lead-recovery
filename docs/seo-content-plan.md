# Raleigh Lead Recovery SEO Content Plan

Use the blog to answer narrow, practical questions that Raleigh plumbing owners already care about: missed calls, after-hours calls, call forwarding, job value, and whether an AI intake assistant can be trusted.

## URL Structure

- Blog index: `/blog`
- Posts: `/blog/[slug]`
- Keep all early content under `/blog`. Do not create separate guide hubs until there are at least 15-20 strong posts around one topic.

## Content Pillars

### 1. Missed Calls

Goal: capture plumbers who already feel the pain of calls going unanswered.

Starter topics:

- What happens when plumbing calls go to voicemail?
- How many missed calls does a small plumbing shop get each week?
- Why urgent plumbing callers rarely wait for voicemail.
- What to do when the owner cannot answer the phone.

### 2. Call Forwarding

Goal: explain that customers keep calling the same number.

Starter topics:

- Missed-call forwarding for plumbers: how it works.
- Busy-line forwarding vs. no-answer forwarding.
- How to forward only after-hours plumbing calls.
- How to test call forwarding before changing your phone setup.

### 3. Lead Value

Goal: help owners put a conservative number on missed-call recovery.

Starter topics:

- How much is a missed plumbing call worth?
- Pipeline value vs. booked revenue for home-service calls.
- How to measure a 7-day missed-call audit.
- What counts as a good plumbing lead?

### 4. Trust And Operations

Goal: answer objections before they come up in sales.

Starter topics:

- Can an AI assistant answer plumbing calls without quoting prices?
- What information should a plumbing intake collect?
- Should plumbing calls be recorded?
- How to handle emergency plumbing calls after hours.

### 5. Local Raleigh Content

Goal: keep the brand local and personal.

Starter topics:

- Missed-call recovery for Raleigh plumbers.
- Emergency plumbing calls in Raleigh: what callers expect.
- Call answering options for Cary and Apex plumbing shops.
- After-hours call handling for Triangle service businesses.

## Video Content

Record short videos first, then turn each into a blog post with transcript, summary, and CTA.

### Video 1: Call The Demo

Hook:

> Raleigh plumbers: call this number like you have a leak.

Outline:

1. Say who it is for.
2. Show the demo number: 984-299-2155.
3. Ask them to pretend they have a backed-up drain, leaking water heater, or no-hot-water issue.
4. Explain that the same flow can catch calls their shop misses.
5. Offer the free 7-day audit.

### Video 2: Your Number Stays The Same

Hook:

> You do not need a new customer-facing phone number to test missed-call recovery.

Outline:

1. Explain missed-call forwarding.
2. Show the normal shop number ringing first.
3. Explain busy, unanswered, and after-hours routing.
4. Explain that it can be turned off.

### Video 3: What The Report Shows

Hook:

> Here is what a plumber sees after seven days of missed-call recovery.

Outline:

1. Show the sample report.
2. Point to calls answered.
3. Point to urgent calls.
4. Explain pipeline value, not closed revenue.
5. Ask if they want to run it on their shop calls.

## Post Template

Every post should include:

- One clear target query.
- Direct answer in the first paragraph.
- A practical example for a plumbing shop.
- A section that addresses one common objection.
- A short call to action for the free 7-day audit.
- Last updated date.
- If video exists, embed it and include the transcript on the same page.

## Internal Linking Rules

- Every blog post should link back to the free audit section on the homepage.
- Every missed-call post should link to the call-forwarding guide.
- Every value post should link to the 7-day audit explanation.
- Every video post should include a transcript and link to at least two written articles.

## Publishing Rhythm

Simple weekly rhythm:

1. Publish one written article.
2. Record one 45-90 second video from the article.
3. Add the video embed to the article after upload.
4. Post the clip on Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, and YouTube Shorts.
5. Send the article or video to 5 relevant plumber prospects when it answers a question they likely have.
