## Targeting notes

- **Best pilot fits first**: family-owned local shops (Bar-Co, AnyDay, Redmond, Richardson, Mitchell,
  Southern Seasons, Jay's, Icy Hot, Sloan) — the owner answers email and can say yes to a free evening test.
- **Larger/franchise operations** (Michael & Son, ARS/Rescue Rooter, One Hour/Green Horizon, Yellow Dot)
  have call centers already; the missed-call pitch is weaker, but the lead-reactivation pitch may still land.
- Weather Master, Newcomb, and Blanton's are established Triangle names — long sales cycles, good case-study
  value if one converts.
