# Customer lifecycle email templates

Smartlead is for prospecting. These messages are for people who already created a Raleigh Lead Recovery account and should be sent by the product email provider (Supabase/Resend), not from the outbound Smartlead mailboxes.

## Welcome — send after onboarding

**Subject:** Your Raleigh Lead Recovery trial is live

Your account is ready. The next step is a short pilot setup so we can review your first 25 contacts, confirm the calling rules, and make sure the list is eligible before anything launches.

**Book your pilot setup:** https://calendly.com/hello-raleighaiguy/raleigh-lead-recovery-pilot-setup

You can also open your dashboard and upload the first 25 contacts whenever you’re ready.

— Danny

## Reminder — 24 hours after signup if no call is booked

**Subject:** Want help getting the first 25 contacts ready?

Your free pilot is waiting. Send a small approved list and I’ll help you review it before the first campaign runs.

**Book pilot setup:** https://calendly.com/hello-raleighaiguy/raleigh-lead-recovery-pilot-setup

## List received

**Subject:** We received your pilot list

I received your contacts. I’m reviewing eligibility, suppression, and the approved calling rules now. I’ll let you know what is ready and what needs to be excluded before launch.

## Campaign ready

**Subject:** Your pilot is ready for review

The first recovery campaign is configured. Please review the audience and calling rules in your dashboard, then book a quick setup call if you want me to walk through it with you.
